FOC Motor Controller

PCB DesignElectronicsMotor

A two-motor field-oriented-control board, rebuilt properly after a rushed first prototype in MIT's 2.679.

I started this during 2.679 my junior spring but wasn't really able to finish it. The prototype came together in far too little time and ended up an incredibly chopped board. I'm redoing it now with more rigorous part selection and an actual end goal in mind.

Goal

Build a board that drives two motors under field-oriented control — this time choosing parts deliberately and keeping documentation as I go, rather than reconstructing it afterwards. The original design was based on ODrive 3.5, the last open-source revision.

Targets

  • Power — 18–40 V input bus (24 V and 36 V nominal), 10 A continuous, 30 A peak
  • Microcontroller — STM32G474RET6, up from the G474RBT6 for 512 KB of flash
  • Communication — CAN bus for control and bootloading, SPI to the encoders
  • Encoders — 2× AS5047D, with headroom reserved for two more off-board

Gate drivers and the power stage are still being decided. The build logs below track those calls as I make them.
