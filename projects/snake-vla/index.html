Snake VLA

Machine LearningRobotics

Building on my work with building reinforcement and imitation learning in snake, I built a basic (definition somewhat arguable) VLA

I am going to be working at Physical Intelligence this fall. As a result, I felt that the work of building my own robotic model would be interest. I also have become generally more interested in doing this and thought it would be a good idea.

As a result, I decided that I would take the basics of my previous implentations to learn the basics of ML to learn how to set up a model that is a VLA.

Now, the definition of a vision-language-action model (VLA) is somewhat up for debate. I have looked online and in a lot of ways have been unsatisfied by the lack of a distinct / definitive answer.

Here are potential thoughts:

Wikipedia: "A vision–language–action model (VLA) is a class of multimodal foundation models that integrates vision, language and actions."

VLA Meta Study Paper from Cornell: "VLA models are multimodal artificial intelligence systems that unify visual perception, language comprehension, and physical action generation into a single framework."

I think most other things I said had this same similar wording but a lot of times the idea of VLAs being a strict "robot-model" were used. While I understand this is the main use, I don't have personal hardware set up to work with my own VLA (yet...) so this will be an intermediary step.

By the end of this project, my goal will be to have done a sufficient literary analysis and built sufficient understanding of common AI / robotic models that my mostly self-constructed personal VLA will be able to achieve snake abilities that will be at least 80% as good as my A* snake aware expert.
