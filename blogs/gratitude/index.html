Gratitude

I find myself so grateful

Recently on my mind has been the concept of gratitude.

For a long time, and still somewhat true, I was motivated by a feeling of competitiveness.

I grew up with three older brothers and always did my best to compete or match them. Whether it was wrestling at home when we were young or making sure that I had the best grades and teachers liked me the most, for a long time I felt driven by a desire to be perceived as better than those around me.

For a while now, I've felt this feeling start to fail. While I still feel competitive and many of the underlying feelings that contribute to this—insecurity, jealousy, fear—still exist in me, I have begun looking to other sources of inspiration and motivation.

Recently, this has come from peers. I think back on the goat, Jack, who I ran the Ihopman with and I can't help but admire his drive as a person to continue to be better. A particular quote has stuck with me. "Why be mean when you can be kind?" I'm not the best at having this motivate all thoughts and actions on a moment-to-moment basis but it definitely has stuck with me.

So I'm trying to be more kind and appreciative for the things around me. And there's so much to be grateful for.

I am (generally) in amazing health. (My hrv and heart rate semm to be lowkey cooked rn). I have had the opportunity to travel and live in various countries and states and experienced an upbringing that most don't have the privilege to. I have seven siblings and a family that developed so many of the traits that I love about myself.

I go to the best university in the world (sometimes in rankings, always in my mind). I've met people from all over who inspire me to work harder and also to enjoy time away from work. Since going, I've been able to scuba dive, teach in Italy, travel to a few other countries, explore other crazy colleges, work with some super smart people, and so so much more.

Most recently, I was given a chance to work at one of the coolest robotics companies in the world. This is partially the reason for a lack of project posts (I swear things are on my mind and getting some work in the background). Regardless, it's cool. Aside from that, I've been able to meet a lot of people since coming to SF! They're smart and work out and love a sort of "grind" when it comes to work which is awesome.

I’m realizing that I really feel this on a good run it a particularly successful day at work. I guess I could describe it as a runners high or flow but recently I’ve just felt such clarity and calm. In moments like those, it seems that the universe has decided that I am worthy of all its blessings.
