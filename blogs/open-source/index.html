Open Source and Open Development

Why do people make it harder to interact with their products?

I am currently in a bit of a conundrum. I typically have Strava statistics on my website, but Strava recently announced a close to their free-to-use API. Now, people have to pay to use the data. Since I'm a big believer in not having to pay in things, especially stuff that is basically mine, I am currently looking into other options.

I have a Garmin Forerunner 265 (a fantastic Christmas present from my gf last year) and I have heard that there is an open source project, python-garminconnect that is able to collect some of the data off the Garmin and allow it to be used with personal projects.

I am realizing more and more that I care much more about the extent to which I am personally able to interact with something "under the hood". I have swapped over to Linux and set up my home server recently. I am using Actual Budget as opposed to things I have used in the past like Rocket Money. I think it's 1) fun to set up and 2) nice because I am aware of the autonomy I have with my data and services.

I am surely not unique here and I think this will be a more and more important thing as time progresses. The programming accessibility created by capable AI agents means more and more people will want the ability to interact with the tools and apps that they use on a regular basis.

I hope the wave comes sooner. Ideally before I set up the garmin connect because I liked that it was already set up :/.
