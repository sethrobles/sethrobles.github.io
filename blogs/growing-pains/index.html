Growing Pains

Changes can be hard

It's my second week in San Francisco. I'm working at one of the coolest companies in the world (Physical Intelligence)[https://www.pi.website/] right now with a fantastic team to learn from. I'm hoping to learn as much as possible while here. In fact, I have decided that if I finish my intern project as I've imagined it, then I'll believe that I'm cracked". In so many ways, I'm living in a reality that I would have been so grateful for just a few months ago. It seems like a lot of my hard work has paid off. But somehow, it doesn't feel as nice as I thought it would.

A few months ago, I thought I would be starting off my second-to-last semester at MIT. I was ready for another semester of research at the Marine Autonomy Lab, more runs around the Esplanade, IM games with AC Baker, late-night drives around Boston, build nights at MITERs, late-night psets at the STUD. I would maybe take 2.009 with my

I'm getting texts from my friend's group chat. I'm hearing about frat rush events, meal plans, and dates being planned. Right now, it doesn't seem that bad. But I'm realizing this is just the first of 14 weeks. So it'll be 14 weeks more where I'm not there. Weekends with friends that I'm not going to be able to make.

Beyond that, there are other struggles. The last few days I've really just had to convince myself that the path I choose for myself is going to pay off in the long run.

Maybe I'll look back at this at the end of my internship or the end of my senior year and I'll think about how much I've grown. Right now though, I just am feeling a lot of the pain.
